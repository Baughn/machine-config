copy-layer name=A to=B
  Copy layer A (pixels, opacity, blend) into a new layer B directly above
  it. B is visible and becomes the current layer. Use it to show a hidden
  underpainting as a solid base under the marks (then `blur` it or move it
  with `layer name=B below=...`), or to glaze a duplicate.
  e.g. copy-layer name=under to=base
