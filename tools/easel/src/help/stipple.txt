stipple in=SHAPE (count=N | density=D | spacing=PX) [placement=random|even] [falloff=PX] [weight=dark(L)|light(L)|alpha(L)] [gamma=G] + mark parameters
  Scatter marks over the part of a shape that lies on the canvas.
  - count=N candidates, or density=D candidates per 1000 px^2 of that area.
  - placement=random (default): uniform. Extremely thin slivers may not
    get every candidate; the report then says how many were not placed.
    placement=even: Poisson-disk blue noise (no clumps). Its minimum
    distance is spacing=PX; if omitted it is derived from count/density
    (the report prints it) and the result is
    thinned randomly to exactly the target. With placement=even,
    spacing= alone is allowed: every point that fits is used.
  - falloff=PX: keep probability ramps from 0 at the shape edge to 1 at PX
    inside: soft-edged clouds.
  - weight=dark(L): keep probability (1 - lightness)^gamma * alpha of layer
    L at the point (lightness = OKLab L, 0 black .. 1 white) -- tonal
    stippling from an underpainting. light(L) uses lightness^gamma * alpha,
    alpha(L) uses alpha^gamma. gamma= (default 1; needs weight=) above 1
    concentrates marks in the darkest/lightest areas.
  - clip= (see marks) also filters candidates.
  Candidates are placed in random order, so later marks do not sweep
  across the shape. The report lists survivors and rejections by reason.
  e.g. stipple in=ellipse(300,330,160,210) density=20 placement=even color=#1a1020 weight=dark(under) gamma=2 r=0.6..1.1
  e.g. stipple in=circle(200,200,80) count=3000 falloff=40 colors=#fff*3,#fd8 r=0.5..1.5 alpha=0.3..0.8
